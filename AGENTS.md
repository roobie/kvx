## Glossary

### Tech debt

**Tech debt** is the future cost of rework and maintenance that comes from choosing a quick, easy or temporary fix in place of a complete, high-quality solution. It accrues interest: the longer the underlying fix waits, the slower, harder and costlier later changes become.

The cost (in time or money, for example) to build something is not tech debt.

**ALWAYS consider tech debt when designing solutions** - low tech debt is always preferable.
